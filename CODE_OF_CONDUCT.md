# BustaCode Code of Conduct

BustaCode welcomes developers of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique ideas rather than people; make reports reproducible; respect different experience levels; keep conversations relevant; and protect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, personal attacks and repeated disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and moderation

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests and community discussions. Maintainers may remove inappropriate material, lock discussions or limit participation to keep the community constructive. Disagreeing with a technical decision in good faith is not a violation.

Report platform harassment using GitHub's **Report content** option. If a private contact method is available on the [project owner's profile](https://github.com/sensodent), use it for confidential community concerns; do not publish private personal details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this public reporting process.

Please help keep BustaCode a respectful place to exchange feedback and ideas.
